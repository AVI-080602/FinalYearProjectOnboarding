# Venue hours: research notes (curation evidence)

Research date: 2026-08-09. Method: official venue/parish/operator websites only,
hours recorded solely when stated on a fetched page. Google APIs not used.
These notes document why 20 of the 47 Tier 1 refuges have no published hours;
in the app they show "hours unknown" rather than a guess.

## Notes on recorded hours worth knowing

- St Paul's Cathedral: Sunday visiting hours not stated (services); recorded Mon-Sat only.
- St Johns Southgate: chapel opened on a voluntary basis during office hours; Wednesday staffing may vary.
- Arts Centre Melbourne: hours are for the Theatres Building; Hamer Hall differs; foyers extend around performances.
- ACMI, Koorie Heritage Trust, Chinese Museum: closed on some or all public holidays.
- OSM-prefilled venues (13) are marked "OpenStreetMap (unverified)" in the CSV; verify against official sites when time permits.
